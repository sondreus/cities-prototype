Source & calculation

WorldPop 2026 population estimates, R2024B v1, unconstrained and UN-adjusted. Native resolution: 30 arcseconds (approximately 1 km at the equator). Values are people per cell; blank cells are source NoData, including water. Cell counts are divided by their geodesic area to obtain people per square kilometre. The same density colour scale is used for every city.

The radius is measured on the WGS84 ellipsoid, from the reference coordinates shown above. Each native cell contributes its population multiplied by its estimated area fraction inside 10 km. Boundary overlap uses a 32 × 32 grid of subcell samples with geodesic area weights, assuming uniform population within each cell. Totals are rounded to the nearest thousand and represent modelled estimates.

Reference points are illustrative city coordinates, not administrative boundaries or a ranking of the twenty largest cities. Moving a reference point would change the total. Each view uses a local azimuthal equidistant projection; the 10 km circle preserves distance from its centre. The raster crop extends about 38 km from the point; panning is limited to this local view.
